# KJ7T

I'm Tom Salzer, callsign KJ7T, grid CN87. Conservationist, sailor, geologist, and amateur radio operator. By day I lead conservation district work across Washington State. On the air, you'll find me active on AllStarLink, DMR, D-STAR, M17, and Yaesu System Fusion, along with HF, packet, and APRS. This site documents the stations and nodes behind the callsign, along with the projects I'm building to make ham radio a little more approachable for the next generation of operators.

- **Mobile:** _2023 Honda Ridgeline — Yaesu FTM-300DR (VFO B runs APRS)_
